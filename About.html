About XAY XUA

Our Story

XAY XUA began with a simple passion: creating unique LEGO builds that push the boundaries of what's possible with these iconic bricks.

What started as a hobby in a small basement has grown into a dedicated workshop where we design, test, and perfect custom LEGO sets that you won't find anywhere else.

Our mission is to share our love for creative building with fellow enthusiasts, collectors, and anyone who appreciates the art of LEGO construction. Each set we offer is meticulously designed to provide both an engaging building experience and a stunning final display piece.
