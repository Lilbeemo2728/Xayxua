About Us

Xay Xua is a non-profit project founded by a group of students from High School for Gifted Students, Hanoi National University of Education. Our mission is to ignite a passion for LEGO and share building experiences with the wider community. In addition, with the goal of raising funds to help preserve unique landmarks in Hanoi, Xay Xua designs and plans to sell LEGO-inspired products based on these very locations.
